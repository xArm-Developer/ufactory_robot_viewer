# Packing example scene

`scene.xml` is generated by `scripts/build-packing-model.py` from this project's
`public/models/xarm6/xarm6.urdf` and `collision.json`. Their upstream sources and
licenses are recorded in `public/models/xarm6/SOURCE.md`. The belt, box, 30 mm
and 17 g blocks, and simplified two-jaw contact geometry are project-authored.

The generator and browser controller share geometry, feed, grip and trajectory
settings in `src/packing-config.json`. The physical model uses MuJoCo 3.15.0, joint position
actuators with URDF effort limits, gravity compensation, and independent sliding
actuators approximating the G2 pad surfaces. A geom `surfacevel` drives the belt
by friction. Contact priorities separate belt, pad and landing parameters. The
parts have free joints and no attachment or adhesion. Parameters are illustrative rather
than calibrated hardware specifications. The visual model uses the separate
project Gripper G2 asset; its source and licenses are in
`public/models/gripper-g2/SOURCE.md`.
